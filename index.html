Frequently Asked Questions About Customer Churn

Everything you need to know about calculating churn rate and client churn analysis.

Calculating churn rate is straightforward: divide the number of customers lost during a specific time period (usually a month) by the number of total customers at the start of that period, then multiply by 100. Formula: (Customers Lost ÷ Starting Customers) × 100.
Customer churn analysis generally applies to high-volume B2C or SaaS subscription businesses where individual ARPU is lower and volume is higher. Client churn analysis is typical for B2B agencies and high-ticket service companies where losing even 1 or 2 clients creates a significant financial impact.
For B2B SaaS and agency retainers, a monthly churn rate below 2%–3.5% is considered healthy. For high-velocity B2C subscriptions and e-commerce memberships, monthly churn typically sits between 5.5% and 8%. Anything above these thresholds indicates onboarding friction or lifecycle drop-offs.
The fastest ways to reduce churn rate include: 1) Targeting higher-intent search traffic on Google/SEO rather than low-intent clicks, 2) Precision Meta Ads remarketing to re-engage inactive accounts, 3) Improving customer onboarding, and 4) Fixing billing friction with automated credit card dunning sequences.
